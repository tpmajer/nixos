# RTL8156 Ethernet card: no wakeups from it

The Framework 2.5G Ethernet card (Realtek RTL8156, 0bda:8156, on the xHCI at
0000:c3:00.4) resumed the machine about 1 s into s2idle, but only with a cable
attached: the port loses power, the PHY sees the link change and signals a USB
remote wakeup, which surfaces as a PCIe PME on 0000:00:08.3 (IRQ 40). With the
lid shut logind adds its 30 s HoldoffTimeoutSec and the two become a
suspend/resume loop: 928 cycles over the night of 2026-09-21. With
`power/wakeup` set to disabled by hand a suspend held
(debug-session-2026-09-22.md).

## Why a hook and not a udev rule

r8152 sets that flag itself: at probe from the chip's WoL bits, and to
"enabled" on every runtime suspend whatever WoL says. The udev rule that was
here until 2026-10-06 was undone within a second of boot and was dead for two
weeks with nothing to show it. TLP's `WOL_DISABLE` only covers PCI NICs
(debug-session-2026-10-06.2.md).

## What `scripts/sleep-pre.sh` does

- Finds the card by its USB ID, not by its driver: as cdc_ncm, its other USB
  configuration, it has no WoL to turn off, but the flag is cleared anyway.
- Acts only with a link. Without one there is no link change to wake on, and
  the card is left autosuspended: no USB resume on this xHCI, the one of the
  s2idle hang, on the way to every sleep. A link that comes up during the sleep
  can then wake the laptop once; the next suspend finds the link and turns the
  wakeup off.
- Sets `power/control=on`, which keeps the card from autosuspending between
  the hook and the suspend and so from setting the flag again. `sleep-post.sh`
  puts it back to `auto`. This used to rest on the 20 s autosuspend delay,
  which is nixos-hardware's and not this repo's.
- Says in the journal (`sleep-actions.service`) what failed, and when the flag
  is not "disabled" in the end.

Cost: the laptop cannot be woken over Ethernet, which is not in use.

The card can also come up on USB 2.0 (480M) instead of USB 3, which caps it at
about 350 Mb/s: check `device/../speed` of the interface.
